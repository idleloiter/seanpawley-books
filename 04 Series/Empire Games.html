Empire Games

Series problem

The represented Empire Games novels turn world-walking from a dynastic smuggling advantage into state infrastructure. Rival governments use surveillance, biotechnology, diplomacy, industrial policy, and inherited family ties to control movement between timelines. The core question is whether states can absorb that capability without making people such as Rita Douglas and Miriam Burgeson into strategic property.

Represented local volumes

  • Empire Games
  • Dark State

Invisible Sun, the concluding volume, is not locally represented. The hub records the position at the end of Dark State without inventing the missing resolution.

Series-scale movement

Empire Games establishes two maturing para-time state systems. The United States converts genome records, coercive recruitment, and reconnaissance technology into the Office of Special Programs; the New American Commonwealth converts imported knowledge and Clan survival into ministries, factories, transport, and military capability. Rita's recruitment connects those systems through a family relationship neither side treats as private.

Dark State deepens the institutional contest. Diplomatic contact, royal succession, the Wolf Orchestra, Camp Singularity, and the Forerunner gateway show that the security problem is no longer only concealment between known timelines. Each government must decide what legal limits, if any, survive when world-walkers are intelligence assets and an older technological power may be present.

The represented movement is therefore from discovery and reconnaissance toward recognition and escalation. Family sovereignty, bureaucratic jurisdiction, and geopolitical survival become aspects of the same problem.
