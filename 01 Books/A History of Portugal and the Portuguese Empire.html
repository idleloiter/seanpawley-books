Work-level patterns

The two represented volumes support a clear division of labor. Volume 1: Portugal explains the kingdom's geography, dynastic formation, municipal institutions, church power, fiscal limits, and European constraints. Volume 2: The Portuguese Empire follows the maritime system through islands, ports, forts, missions, private traders, colonial settlements, and overseas coercion. Together they make Portugal useful for comparing how a small state converted maritime gateways into leverage while remaining constrained by manpower, finance, and contested authority. The strongest hub-level concepts are Maritime Power, Empire and Periphery, Commercial Society, Financial Infrastructure, and State Capacity.
