Series-level patterns

The represented volume notes support a series-level pattern around logistics, periphery, and legitimacy. Leviathan Wakes shows a brittle solar order through policing, extraction, and hidden experiment; Caliban's War makes military escalation and food-system vulnerability visible; Abaddon's Gate turns the ring into a gateway and bottleneck; Cibola Burn makes frontier settlement and chartered force concrete; Babylon's Ashes tests whether grievance can become governable order. The strongest hub-level concepts are Empire and Periphery, Frontier Systems, Logistics and Throughput, Speculative Governance, and Information and Coordination.
