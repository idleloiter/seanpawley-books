Series-level patterns

The represented volumes support a concise series-level claim: Stephenson treats modernity as a coupled system of science, credit, statecraft, commerce, and elite coordination. Quicksilver introduces the entanglement of natural philosophy, court politics, and money; The Confusion widens the field through trade, war, and movement; The System of the World turns those pressures toward settlement and institutional hardening. The hub-level concepts that earn their place are Financial Infrastructure, Commercial Society, Capital Allocation, Technological Change, and Elite Formation.
