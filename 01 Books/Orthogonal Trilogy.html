Series-level patterns

The represented volumes make the trilogy useful for comparing how physical substrate constrains political imagination. The Clockwork Rocket builds science, labor, reproductive order, and the Peerless project from unfamiliar optics and biology; The Arrows of Time turns those physical rules into problems of memory, foreknowledge, return, and institutional choice. The hub-level comparison therefore belongs with Ecological Constraint, Technological Change, Knowledge Preservation, and Speculative Governance.
