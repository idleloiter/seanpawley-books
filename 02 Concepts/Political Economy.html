Political Economy

This is a holding note for overly broad political-economy links. Use narrower concepts first: Commercial Society for market society, Capital Allocation for investment choices, Industrial Policy for state-led capability building, Financial Infrastructure for monetary and credit rails, and State Capacity for administrative execution.

Use a narrower concept when one mechanism dominates. When production, power, finance, and distribution must be analyzed together, route the link to Economy, Firms, and Infrastructure. Reserve Common Patterns and Transferable Lessons for explicit synthesis across economic and non-economic domains.
