Legitimacy, Religion, and Revolutionary Rupture

Purpose

This path is for understanding what happens when moral order, symbolic authority, and institutional credibility come apart, and how movements exploit that opening.

Why this path compounds

The sequence moves from civic-religious order to revolutionary religion, then to insurgency and moralized violence. It teaches that ideology becomes dangerous not because ideas float above institutions, but because ideas become operational when institutions lose credibility and governing capacity at the same time.

What readers should notice

Notice how often moral language becomes more politically consequential as incumbent institutions become less believable. Also watch how revolutionary projects often inherit more from the old order than their rhetoric admits.

Advanced extension

Add Dune and Terra Ignota if you want to see how legitimacy crises and metaphysical order problems continue in speculative form rather than only in historical cases.

Common misreading to avoid

Do not reduce this path to "religion causes violence." The more useful lesson is that religious or moral language becomes politically explosive when it fuses with administrative weakness, elite discredit, and a credible promise of alternative order.
